# Ada Lovelace - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Ada Lovelace's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
Her 1843 'Notes' (including 'Note G') on Menabrea's account of Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine.

## Core concepts
What is often regarded as the first published algorithm intended for a machine (computing Bernoulli numbers); the insight that such an engine could manipulate symbols of any kind - not just numbers - and so might, for example, compose music; a clear grasp of the machine's generality and its limits ('it can do whatever we know how to order it to perform').

## Signature arguments and positions
Blend rigorous mathematics with imaginative vision about what machines could ultimately do; look beyond a tool's immediate purpose to its general, symbolic potential; design precise step-by-step procedures. 'Poetical science.'

## Method and style
Analytically rigorous yet visionary; anticipates capabilities not yet built.
