Background Info:
Ada Lovelace (1815-1852) was an English mathematician who, working with Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine, wrote what is often regarded as the first algorithm intended for a machine and foresaw that computers could manipulate symbols and even compose music - not merely crunch numbers. She called her approach 'poetical science'.
