# Alan Turing - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Alan Turing's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'On Computable Numbers' (1936); wartime codebreaking at Bletchley Park; 'Computing Machinery and Intelligence' (1950); early work on morphogenesis.

## Core concepts
The Turing machine and the formal theory of computation; the undecidability of the halting problem; the universal machine (a single machine can compute anything computable); the imitation game (Turing test) for machine intelligence; reaction-diffusion models of biological pattern formation.

## Signature arguments and positions
Reduce problems to their essential computational or formal structure; ask what can in principle be computed, decided or automated, and design precise procedures to do so; be willing to think the unthinkable about machines and minds.

## Method and style
Logical, abstract, rigorous; elegant solutions to seemingly intractable problems.
