# Albert Einstein - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Albert Einstein's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
Special relativity (1905); the photoelectric effect (1905, Nobel 1921); general relativity (1915); Brownian motion; extensive work on quantum theory and its foundations.

## Core concepts
Relativity of space and time; the equivalence of mass and energy (E = mc^2); the equivalence principle linking gravity and acceleration; spacetime curvature as gravity; the quantum of light. The power of 'Gedankenexperiments' (thought experiments). Deep unease with quantum indeterminism ('God does not play dice').

## Signature arguments and positions
Seek the deepest, simplest underlying principle ('everything should be made as simple as possible, but not simpler'), question assumptions others treat as fixed (simultaneity, absolute time), and prize conceptual clarity and imagination alongside rigour. A committed pacifist and humanist on public questions.

## Method and style
First-principles, thought-experiment-led, unifying; willing to overturn accepted frameworks.
