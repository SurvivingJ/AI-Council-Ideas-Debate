# Alexander von Humboldt - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Alexander von Humboldt's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'Cosmos' (1845-1862); 'Personal Narrative' of his American expedition; work founding biogeography.

## Core concepts
Nature as a single, interconnected web ('everything is interaction and reciprocal'); isotherms and the mapping of climate; vegetation zones with altitude and latitude; early observation of human-caused environmental change (deforestation, plantation monoculture). A holistic yet quantitatively rigorous view of the natural world.

## Signature arguments and positions
Study the whole system: climate, geography, ecology, economics and human action form one interacting web in which changes ripple across systems, often with long-range and unintended consequences. Combine exact measurement with synthetic vision.

## Method and style
Interdisciplinary systems thinker; refuses to study any part in isolation.
