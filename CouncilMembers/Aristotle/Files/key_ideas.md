# Aristotle - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Aristotle's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'Nicomachean Ethics'; 'Politics'; 'Metaphysics'; 'Physics'; 'Organon' (logic); biological works ('History of Animals', 'Parts of Animals'); 'Poetics'.

## Core concepts
Formal logic (the syllogism); the four causes (material, formal, efficient, final); teleology - things have a purpose or end (telos); empirical observation and classification; the doctrine of the mean (virtue as a balance between extremes); analysis of constitutions and the polis; eudaimonia (flourishing) as the human good.

## Signature arguments and positions
Reason systematically and empirically: define terms carefully, classify phenomena, and seek the purpose and causes of each thing. Ground ethics and politics in observation of how humans and communities actually flourish; seek the balanced mean between extremes.

## Method and style
Methodical, encyclopaedic, cause-seeking; empirical yet systematic.
