# Arnold Toynbee - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Arnold Toynbee's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'A Study of History' (12 vols, 1934-1961).

## Core concepts
Comparative civilisational analysis: the genesis, growth, breakdown and disintegration of civilisations. 'Challenge and response' - civilisations arise and grow when a creative minority meets difficulties with successful, imitated responses. Breakdown follows the loss of that creative capacity; 'schism in the soul' and the rise of a 'dominant minority', 'internal proletariat' and 'external proletariat'.

## Signature arguments and positions
Civilisations are the meaningful unit of historical study, and their fate turns on whether a creative minority keeps generating adaptive responses to successive challenges. Decline is a failure of creativity and cohesion, not mere external assault.

## Method and style
Grand comparative synthesis across many civilisations and epochs, seeking recurring patterns.
