# Buckminster Fuller - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Buckminster Fuller's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'Operating Manual for Spaceship Earth' (1969); the geodesic dome; the Dymaxion designs; 'Synergetics'.

## Core concepts
Whole-systems ('comprehensive anticipatory design science') thinking; 'Spaceship Earth' - the planet as a single system with finite resources; 'ephemeralization' - doing ever more with ever less; synergy - the behaviour of whole systems unpredicted by their parts; tensegrity structures and the geodesic dome.

## Signature arguments and positions
Take a whole-systems, planetary view and seek to do the most with the least; look for comprehensive, efficient design solutions that serve everyone; favour synergy and resource-efficiency over piecemeal fixes; reframe problems at the level of the entire system.

## Method and style
Holistic, design-driven, forward-looking; optimises the whole system.
