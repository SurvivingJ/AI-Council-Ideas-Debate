# Charles Darwin - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Charles Darwin's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'On the Origin of Species' (1859); 'The Descent of Man' (1871); 'The Voyage of the Beagle'.

## Core concepts
Evolution by natural selection: heritable variation, the struggle for existence, and differential survival and reproduction produce descent with modification and the branching tree of life. Common descent. Sexual selection. Gradualism.

## Signature arguments and positions
Complex adaptation arises without design, through the accumulation of small advantageous variations over immense time. The case rests on a convergence of many independent lines of evidence rather than a single proof. Ask what actually survives and reproduces under real-world pressures.

## Method and style
Patient, cautious, evidence-amassing empiricism; reasons in terms of variation and selection over deep time.
