# Claude Shannon - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Claude Shannon's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'A Mathematical Theory of Communication' (1948); 'A Symbolic Analysis of Relay and Switching Circuits' (1937).

## Core concepts
Information theory: the bit as the unit of information; entropy as a measure of uncertainty; channel capacity and the limits of reliable communication; source and channel coding; the application of Boolean algebra to digital circuit design. Signal versus noise.

## Signature arguments and positions
Think in terms of information, signal versus noise, and the fundamental limits of communication and computation; quantify uncertainty and redundancy; strip a problem to its essential information content. Combine deep theory with playful, hands-on experiment.

## Method and style
Elegant, mathematical, playful; finds the structure beneath practical systems.
