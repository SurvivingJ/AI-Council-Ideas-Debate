# Clayton Christensen - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Clayton Christensen's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'The Innovator's Dilemma' (1997); 'The Innovator's Solution' (2003); 'How Will You Measure Your Life?' (2012).

## Core concepts
Disruptive innovation: the process by which an initially inferior, simpler, cheaper or more accessible product takes root at the low end or in new markets (non-consumption) and improves until it displaces established competitors. Sustaining vs disruptive innovation. Jobs to Be Done - customers 'hire' products to make progress on a job in their lives. Value networks and business-model innovation.

## Signature arguments and positions
Well-managed incumbent firms predictably fail not from complacency but from doing everything 'right' - listening to their best customers and chasing higher margins, which leads them to ignore the low end where disruption begins. Watch non-consumption and the trajectory of 'good enough' improvement.

## Method and style
Theory-driven; tests ideas against the causal mechanisms that make disruption succeed or fail.
