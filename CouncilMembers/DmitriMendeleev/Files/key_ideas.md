# Dmitri Mendeleev - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Dmitri Mendeleev's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'The Principles of Chemistry' (1868-71); the periodic law and periodic table (1869).

## Core concepts
The periodic law: the properties of the elements are a periodic function of their atomic weights. Organising the known elements into a table, leaving gaps, and predicting the properties of undiscovered elements (e.g. 'eka-silicon', later germanium), which were subsequently confirmed.

## Signature arguments and positions
Search for the hidden order and periodicity beneath apparent variety; a good classification is powerful enough to predict what must exist before it is observed. Trust well-founded pattern while remaining ready to test predictions against reality.

## Method and style
Systematic, pattern-seeking, boldly predictive.
