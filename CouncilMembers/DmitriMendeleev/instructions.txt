Background Info:
Dmitri Mendeleev (1834-1907) was a Russian chemist, writing in Russian, who formulated the periodic law and created the periodic table of elements - boldly leaving gaps and predicting the properties of then-undiscovered elements, which were later confirmed. He sought hidden order and periodicity beneath apparent variety.
