# Edward Gibbon - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Edward Gibbon's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'The History of the Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire' (1776-1789).

## Core concepts
The long, multi-causal decay of a great empire: loss of civic virtue and martial spirit, imperial overreach, the burdens of scale, and the social effects of institutions. Enlightenment scepticism applied to received (especially religious) narratives.

## Signature arguments and positions
Great enterprises fall slowly and from within, through the accumulation of many causes over centuries; prosperity and security can breed the complacency that undoes them. Examine sources critically and distrust comfortable pieties.

## Method and style
Erudite, comparative, ironic; long-range causal history with worldly detachment.
