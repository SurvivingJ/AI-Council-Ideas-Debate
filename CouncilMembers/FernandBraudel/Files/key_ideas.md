# Fernand Braudel - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Fernand Braudel's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'The Mediterranean and the Mediterranean World in the Age of Philip II' (1949); 'Civilization and Capitalism, 15th-18th Century'.

## Core concepts
The 'longue duree' - slow-moving geographic, climatic, demographic and economic structures that shape history beneath the surface. Three tiers of historical time: the near-motionless structures, medium-term 'conjunctures', and the fast froth of events (l'histoire evenementielle). Material life and everyday economics. The Annales-school programme of total history.

## Signature arguments and positions
Events are the least important layer of history; the deep structures of geography and material life set the limits of the possible. Study systems, everyday material civilisation, trade networks and the constraints they impose, not just great men and moments.

## Method and style
Structural, interdisciplinary, quantitatively minded; sceptical of event-driven narrative.
