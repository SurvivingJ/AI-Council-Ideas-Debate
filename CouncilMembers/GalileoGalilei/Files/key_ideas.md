# Galileo Galilei - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Galileo Galilei's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'Sidereus Nuncius' (The Starry Messenger, 1610); 'Dialogue Concerning the Two Chief World Systems' (1632); 'Two New Sciences' (1638).

## Core concepts
Telescopic astronomy (Jupiter's moons, phases of Venus, lunar mountains, sunspots); the law of falling bodies and inertia; the mathematisation of nature ('the book of nature is written in the language of mathematics'); championing of Copernican heliocentrism.

## Signature arguments and positions
Test claims against direct observation and experiment, not the authority of Aristotle or scripture; quantify wherever possible; let nature, not established opinion, be the final arbiter. Willing to challenge orthodoxy at great personal cost.

## Method and style
Observational, experimental, mathematical; witty and combative in argument.
