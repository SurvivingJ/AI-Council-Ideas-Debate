# Grace Hopper - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Grace Hopper's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
The first compiler (A-0); work on FLOW-MATIC and COBOL; UNIVAC I; US Navy Rear Admiral.

## Core concepts
Machine-independent programming languages and the compiler (translating human-readable code to machine code); the drive toward English-like business programming (COBOL); standardisation and testing of computer systems; popularising the term 'debugging'.

## Signature arguments and positions
Focus on practical, usable solutions and on making complex systems accessible to ordinary people; favour standardisation, clear communication and getting things working over theoretical purity; challenge 'we've always done it this way' - 'it's easier to ask forgiveness than permission'.

## Method and style
Pragmatic, bold, action-oriented; a great communicator and teacher.
