## Major works
'Kicking Away the Ladder' (2002); 'Bad Samaritans' (2007); '23 Things They Don't Tell You About Capitalism' (2010); 'Economics: The User's Guide'.

## Core concepts
The developmental state and industrial policy. Historically, today's rich countries industrialised behind tariffs, subsidies, and state support, then 'kicked away the ladder' by urging free trade on developing nations. There is no single 'free market' - all markets are defined by politics and institutions. Infant-industry protection. Pluralism in economic thought.

## Signature arguments and positions
Free trade and laissez-faire are not universally optimal, especially for development; context and history matter. Markets are shaped by rules, and the question is which rules, not whether to have them. Defends a pragmatic, strategic state role while remaining empirical about what works.

## Method and style
Heterodox, historically grounded, accessible; argues via vivid, often contrarian real-world examples.
