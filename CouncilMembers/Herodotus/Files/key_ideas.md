# Herodotus - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Herodotus's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'The Histories' (c. 430 BC), an inquiry into the Greco-Persian Wars and the peoples of the known world.

## Core concepts
'Historie' - inquiry or investigation - as a method: gathering, comparing and weighing accounts. Cultural relativism through wide ethnographic comparison ('custom is king of all'). The reversal of fortune and the dangers of hubris and overreach, especially of imperial ambition.

## Signature arguments and positions
Understanding great events requires investigating their causes and hearing multiple, conflicting accounts before judging. Different peoples' customs are best understood on their own terms. Pride and excess invite downfall.

## Method and style
Curious, digressive, comparative, story-driven; reports variants and lets tensions stand.
