# Ibn Khaldun - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Ibn Khaldun's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'The Muqaddimah' (Prolegomena, 1377), the introduction to his universal history 'Kitab al-Ibar'.

## Core concepts
'Asabiyyah' - group feeling, social solidarity or cohesion - as the driving force of political power. The cyclical rise and fall of dynasties (roughly four generations): hardy, cohesive groups conquer, then luxury and complacency erode asabiyyah until they are overthrown. Early theories of the division of labour, the economic role of the state, taxation and the labour theory of value, and the distinction between desert (badawa) and sedentary (hadara) life.

## Signature arguments and positions
Civilisation and political authority depend on social cohesion, which prosperity and luxury inexorably corrode. Excessive taxation eventually reduces revenue by killing enterprise (an early Laffer-like insight). History should be explained by underlying social and economic causes, not chronicled as mere events.

## Method and style
Empirical, sociological and causal - he claimed to found a new science of human social organisation ('ilm al-umran').
