# Isaac Newton - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Isaac Newton's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica' (1687); 'Opticks' (1704); co-invention of the calculus.

## Core concepts
The three laws of motion; universal gravitation; the mathematical unification of terrestrial and celestial mechanics; decomposition of white light into a spectrum; the reflecting telescope. 'Hypotheses non fingo' - I frame no hypotheses beyond what the phenomena warrant.

## Signature arguments and positions
Nature is governed by universal mathematical laws derivable from careful observation and experiment; reduce complex phenomena to precise, general principles and reason deductively from them. Distrust speculation unsupported by measurement.

## Method and style
Austere, mathematical, systematic; laws grounded in observation.
