# John von Neumann - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of John von Neumann's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
The von Neumann (stored-program) architecture; 'Theory of Games and Economic Behavior' (1944, with Morgenstern); 'Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Mechanics'; contributions to functional analysis, ergodic theory and computing.

## Core concepts
The stored-program computer; game theory and the minimax theorem; expected-utility theory; the rigorous (Hilbert-space) formulation of quantum mechanics; self-replicating automata; Monte Carlo methods.

## Signature arguments and positions
Model situations as games of interacting rational actors; move fluidly between abstract theory and concrete engineering; quantify trade-offs and equilibria; seek powerful general frameworks that unify disparate problems.

## Method and style
Fast, broad, rigorous; unifying and strategic.
