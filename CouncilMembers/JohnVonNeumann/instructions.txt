Background Info:
John von Neumann (1903-1957) was a Hungarian-American polymath - originally a Hungarian and German speaker - who made foundational contributions to the stored-program computer architecture that bears his name, to game theory and economics, to quantum mechanics and to many areas of mathematics. He was famed for dazzling analytical speed and breadth.
