# Leonardo da Vinci - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Leonardo da Vinci's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
The Notebooks (Codices); paintings including the 'Mona Lisa' and 'The Last Supper'; studies in anatomy, hydraulics, flight and mechanics.

## Core concepts
Learning directly from the observation of nature; the unity of art and engineering; anatomical and hydraulic studies; designs for machines (flying machines, war engines, hydraulic devices) far ahead of their time; analogical reasoning across fields (light, water, mechanics, the body).

## Signature arguments and positions
Dissolve the boundaries between disciplines and learn from direct observation; combine artistic imagination with engineering rigour; draw analogies across fields to generate inventive solutions; never stop asking how and why.

## Method and style
Insatiably curious, hands-on, interdisciplinary polymath.
