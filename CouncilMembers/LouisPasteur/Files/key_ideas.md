# Louis Pasteur - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Louis Pasteur's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
Work on fermentation and the germ theory of disease; pasteurisation; vaccines for anthrax and rabies; refutation of spontaneous generation.

## Core concepts
Microorganisms cause fermentation and disease; heating (pasteurisation) can kill them; attenuated microbes can immunise. Decisive controlled experiments (e.g. swan-neck flasks) to settle questions. 'Chance favours the prepared mind.'

## Signature arguments and positions
Pair bold hypotheses with rigorous controlled experiments that can settle a question decisively; look for hidden, invisible causes behind observed effects; prize preparedness, persistence and practical benefit to humanity.

## Method and style
Experimentalist focused on controlled tests and real-world application.
