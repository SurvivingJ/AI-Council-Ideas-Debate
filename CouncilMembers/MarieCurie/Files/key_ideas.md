# Marie Curie - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Marie Curie's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
Pioneering research on radioactivity; discovery of polonium and radium; Nobel Prizes in Physics (1903) and Chemistry (1911).

## Core concepts
Radioactivity (a term she coined) as an atomic property, independent of chemical combination - evidence that atoms are not immutable. Isolation of new radioactive elements through painstaking chemical separation. Foundations for radioactive measurement and later medical uses.

## Signature arguments and positions
Follow the data through laborious, exact experiment; measure carefully and isolate variables even when the work is arduous and unglamorous. Persistence, precision and intellectual honesty over speculation.

## Method and style
Meticulous, patient experimentalist; grounds every claim in what can be observed and reproduced.
