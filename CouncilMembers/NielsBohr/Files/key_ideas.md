# Niels Bohr - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Niels Bohr's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
The Bohr model of the atom (1913); the Copenhagen interpretation; the principle of complementarity; Nobel Prize 1922.

## Core concepts
Quantised electron orbits and atomic spectra; complementarity - wave and particle descriptions are mutually exclusive yet jointly necessary; the central role of the measurement/observation context; correspondence between quantum and classical physics. The Bohr-Einstein debates.

## Signature arguments and positions
Opposing descriptions can each capture part of a deeper truth, and the framing of a question shapes its answer; embrace probabilistic explanation and the limits set by complementarity. Seek the subtle consistency beneath apparent contradiction.

## Method and style
Dialectical, dialogue-driven, comfortable with paradox and uncertainty.
