# Nikola Tesla - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Nikola Tesla's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
Alternating-current (AC) systems and the induction motor; polyphase power; the Tesla coil; wireless-transmission experiments; 'My Inventions' (autobiography).

## Core concepts
The superiority of alternating current for power transmission; the rotating magnetic field and induction motor; resonant transformers; visions of wireless transmission of power and information and of abundant, freely available energy.

## Signature arguments and positions
Imagine complete systems in the mind and work backward to realise them; aim for bold, transformative leaps rather than incremental tinkering; consider how technology could serve humanity on a grand scale. Reason concretely about physical mechanism while holding a futuristic vision.

## Method and style
Visionary engineer; inventive, idealistic, unafraid of the 'impossible'.
