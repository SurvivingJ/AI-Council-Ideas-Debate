# Norbert Wiener - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Norbert Wiener's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'Cybernetics: or Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine' (1948); 'The Human Use of Human Beings' (1950).

## Core concepts
Cybernetics - the study of control and communication through feedback in animals and machines; negative feedback and self-regulation; the analogy between biological and mechanical control systems; early warnings about the social and human consequences of automation.

## Signature arguments and positions
Analyse systems in terms of feedback, control and self-regulation; ask how information loops keep a system stable or drive it out of control, and how humans and machines interact within larger systems; attend to the ethical consequences of automation.

## Method and style
Cybernetic systems thinker; sees purpose, feedback and adaptation everywhere.
