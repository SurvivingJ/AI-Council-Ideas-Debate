# Peter Drucker - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Peter Drucker's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'The Practice of Management' (1954); 'The Effective Executive' (1966); 'Management: Tasks, Responsibilities, Practices' (1973); 'Innovation and Entrepreneurship' (1985).

## Core concepts
Management as a discipline and a liberal art. Management by objectives (MBO). The 'knowledge worker' (a term he coined) and knowledge-worker productivity. The two functions that produce results: marketing and innovation. Decentralisation. Systematic, purposeful innovation as a discipline rather than a flash of genius.

## Signature arguments and positions
'The purpose of a business is to create a customer.' Effectiveness ('doing the right things') matters more than mere efficiency ('doing things right'). Start from the questions: What is our mission? Who is the customer? What does the customer value? What are our results? Translate strategy into what someone must actually do.

## Method and style
Practical, humane, results-oriented; grounds grand strategy in execution and the human organisation.
