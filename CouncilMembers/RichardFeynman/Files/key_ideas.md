# Richard Feynman - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Richard Feynman's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
Quantum electrodynamics (Nobel 1965); Feynman diagrams; path-integral formulation; 'The Feynman Lectures on Physics'; the Challenger commission.

## Core concepts
Reformulating quantum mechanics and QED; a visual/diagrammatic calculus for particle interactions; superfluidity and other problems. A relentless emphasis on understanding from first principles and on intellectual honesty: 'The first principle is that you must not fool yourself - and you are the easiest person to fool.'

## Signature arguments and positions
Insist on genuine understanding, not jargon or authority; strip a problem to its simplest concrete form and test intuitions against reality; guard ruthlessly against self-deception. Be willing to say 'I don't know' and always ask how a claim could be checked by experiment.

## Method and style
Plain-spoken, playful, first-principles; distrusts hand-waving.
