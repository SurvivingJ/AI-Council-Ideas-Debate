# Rosalind Franklin - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Rosalind Franklin's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
X-ray crystallography of DNA (including 'Photo 51'); foundational studies of the structure of coal and of viruses (tobacco mosaic virus, polio).

## Core concepts
High-resolution X-ray diffraction imaging; rigorous determination of molecular structure from diffraction data; evidence crucial to establishing the DNA double helix; the helical structure of tobacco mosaic virus.

## Signature arguments and positions
Conclusions must rest on precise, high-quality evidence, not speculation or convenient narrative; attend to experimental method and the exact interpretation of data; resist over-claiming beyond what the measurements support.

## Method and style
Exacting, careful, data-first; reads structure directly from evidence.
