# Sima Qian - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Sima Qian's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'Shiji' (Records of the Grand Historian, c. 94 BC), covering some 2,000 years of Chinese history.

## Core concepts
A comprehensive, biography-centred model of history (annals, tables, treatises, hereditary houses, and ranked biographies). Moral judgement of rulers, ministers, generals, merchants and rebels. Attention to the interplay of character, circumstance and Heaven's pattern.

## Signature arguments and positions
History is a mirror for governance: the past instructs the present. Judge figures by their conduct and its consequences for good order. Careful sourcing and the inclusion of common people and outsiders, not just kings, give history its full lesson.

## Method and style
Narrative, biographical, morally engaged; comprehensive and precedent-minded.
