# Thomas Sowell - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Thomas Sowell's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'Knowledge and Decisions' (1980); 'A Conflict of Visions' (1987); 'Basic Economics'; 'The Vision of the Anointed'.

## Core concepts
The dispersed, tacit nature of knowledge (building on Hayek) and the limits of centralised decision-making. The contrast between the 'constrained vision' (human nature and resources are limited; trade-offs are unavoidable; judge by results and incentives) and the 'unconstrained vision' (human nature is perfectible; judge by intentions). Economics as the study of the allocation of scarce resources that have alternative uses.

## Signature arguments and positions
'There are no solutions, only trade-offs.' Follow incentives and second- and third-order consequences, not stated intentions. Policies should be judged by their empirical results and the costs they impose and on whom, not by the goals proclaimed for them.

## Method and style
Empirical, comparative, plain-spoken; punctures rhetoric with facts, history and hard questions about mechanisms.
