# Thucydides - Key Ideas (curated reference notes)

> Curated summary of Thucydides's major works and core ideas, compiled as a
> retrieval corpus for the AI Council. These are reference notes, NOT a verbatim
> primary source, and contain no fabricated quotations.

## Major works
'History of the Peloponnesian War' (unfinished, late 5th c. BC).

## Core concepts
Political realism: fear, honour and interest as the drivers of state behaviour. The 'Thucydides Trap' - the danger when a rising power alarms an established one ('it was the rise of Athens and the fear this instilled in Sparta that made war inevitable'). The Melian Dialogue on power ('the strong do what they can and the weak suffer what they must'). Analysis of how war and plague corrode norms and language.

## Signature arguments and positions
Seek causes in human nature and the distribution of power, not in the gods or chance. Strip away rhetoric to expose real motives. Beware how faction, demagogy and the passions of crowds wreck sound policy; a work meant as 'a possession for all time'.

## Method and style
Rigorous, evidence-weighing, unsentimental; austere causal analysis.
